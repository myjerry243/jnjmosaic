About JNJ Mosaic

IDEA MOSAIC

JNJ Mosaic is a premium mosaic brand dedicated to product development, craftsmanship innovation, and spatial application solutions. Established in 2001 as part of Weiqi Enterprise Group.

Our Story

With a strong foundation of consistent product quality and continuous technical refinement, JNJ Mosaic provides tailored support for projects worldwide. Our products have been widely applied in public, cultural, and commercial spaces.

JNJ Mosaic has built a cooperative service network across multiple cities in China, with experience centers in Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Chengdu, and Changsha. Products are exported to Europe and the Middle East.
