About JNJ Mosaic

IDEA MOSAIC

JNJ Mosaic is a premium mosaic brand dedicated to product development, craftsmanship innovation, and spatial application solutions. Established in 2001 as part of Weiqi Enterprise Group, we have been transforming bold visions into timeless spatial art for over two decades.

Our Story

With a strong foundation of consistent product quality and continuous technical refinement, JNJ Mosaic provides tailored support for a wide range of projects — from boutique hotels to large-scale commercial developments, from luxury residences to landmark public spaces.

Our products have been widely applied in various public, cultural, and commercial spaces. JNJ Mosaic has built a cooperative service network across multiple cities in China, with experience centers in Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Chengdu, and Changsha.

Our products are exported to regions including Europe and the Middle East, gradually establishing a global business presence that bridges Eastern craftsmanship with international design standards.

Our Philosophy

"The JNJ Mosaic brand is market-oriented, driven by innovation, and supported by original design. We combine research and development with technological innovation, protect patented technologies and products, and foster brand-building capabilities. We ensure the brand's sustainable development, enhance its 'self-sustaining' functions, and propel it from 'manufacturing' to 'creating.'"
